# Project Deep-dive Template

Do not invent experience, metrics, or contributions. Fill this template with actual materials.

## 90-second introduction

Problem and context → my responsibility → key technical decision → verifiable outcome → most important lesson.

## Technical evidence

| Dimension | Material to provide |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | What I did, what the team did, and what remained out of scope. |
| Architecture | Data flow, interfaces, storage, dependencies, and deployment boundaries. |
| Trade-off | Alternatives, constraints, decision evidence, and rejected options. |
| Outcome | Baseline, measurement method, sample size, uncertainty, and actual metrics. |
| Failure | Reproduction conditions, root cause, fix, and recurrence prevention. |
| Follow-up | Changes needed at 10× scale, during node failures, or with stale data. |

## Rehearse follow-ups

Why this approach? What was technically hardest? What would change if you rebuilt it? How do you attribute the result to your work?
