## Explain without notes

1. Clarify constraints and edge cases.
2. Establish a baseline and justify improvements.
3. Explain the invariant and correctness.
4. Check empty inputs, duplicates, and large cases.
5. Record the cause of mistakes; solve from scratch next time.
